# AutoStore USB HID keyboard sequence (SoftAP SSID AutoStore)
# Format: <delay_seconds> <KEY>
# delay = wait AFTER the previous key before sending this one
#
# Default: open the PS Store tile on the home content row.
# The board only presses keys. It does not rewrite the Store tile.
# Point Store at your own jailbreak host on the console before use.
#
# After console detect, focus is one slot right of Store.
# One LEFT, then Enter.
# Tune delays on your console, then re-upload LittleFS.

0.3 LEFT
0.3 ENTER
